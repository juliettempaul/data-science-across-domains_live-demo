# BHD 301: Introduction to Behavioral and Social Data Science

## Data Science Across the Domains

### Two Kinds of Datasets, One Method — Live Demos

Two small, self-contained CNN teaching demos, both runnable live in-browser via
[Binder](https://mybinder.org) — no Google account, no install. Each launches in a few minutes on
Binder's free CPU-only environment.

### Smile classification

[![Binder](https://mybinder.org/badge_logo.svg)](https://mybinder.org/v2/gh/juliettempaul/data-science-across-domains_live-demo/main?filepath=smile_classification_live_demo.ipynb)

Classifies face images as smiling or not smiling, trained on a small sample (380 images) of the
[GENKI-4K dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/talhasar/genki4k).

### Marginalia classification

[![Binder](https://mybinder.org/badge_logo.svg)](https://mybinder.org/v2/gh/juliettempaul/data-science-across-domains_live-demo/main?filepath=marginalia_classification_live_demo.ipynb)

Classifies scanned pages of early modern printed books as containing handwritten marginalia or not,
trained on a small sample (198 images).
